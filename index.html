10-Screen Verified Low-Fidelity Journey

The hand-drawn wireframes created for CP5 form the foundational architecture of HostelFix. Each screen directly solves a verified user pain point identified during student interviews:

  • Screens 1 & 2: Quick college email login & room mapping to prevent anonymous spam.
  • Screen 3: 4-Quadrant Home screen providing immediate 1-click access to Report Complaint.
  • Screens 4 & 5: Standardized categorization + instant unique Reference Token #HF1087.
  • Screen 6: 4-Stage vertical progress timeline showing exactly who owns the repair.
  • Screen 7: Visit window advance notices to eliminate "Room Locked" wasted visits.
  • Screens 8 & 9: Streamlined Warden triaging and technician work-order logging.
  • Screen 10: Mandatory two-way resolution validation with 1-click reopen escalation.
